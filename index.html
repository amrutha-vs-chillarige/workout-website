💪 Beginner Standing Resistance Band Workouts

Quick 10–15 Minute 4-Day Split

Timer: 00:00

Day 1: Upper Body (Push)

Chest Press – 12–15 reps (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)
Anchor the band behind you (around a door or sturdy pole). Hold handles at chest height, elbows bent. Push arms forward until straight, then return slowly.
Shoulder Press – 12–15 reps (Shoulders, Triceps)
Stand on the band with feet shoulder-width apart. Hold handles at shoulder height, palms forward. Press upward until arms are straight, then lower slowly.

Day 2: Lower Body

Band Squats – 12–15 reps (Quads, Glutes)
Stand on the band with feet shoulder-width apart. Hold handles at shoulders. Sit back into a squat, keeping chest up, then push through heels to stand.
Side Steps – 12–15 each side (Glutes, Hips)
Place band around ankles. Bend knees slightly. Step sideways with control, keeping tension in the band, then step back.

Day 3: Upper Body (Pull)

Band Rows – 12–15 reps (Back, Biceps)
Anchor band at chest height. Hold handles with arms straight. Pull elbows back, squeezing shoulder blades together, then return slowly.
Bicep Curl – 12–15 reps (Biceps)
Stand on the band with feet hip-width apart. Hold handles with palms up. Curl hands toward shoulders, then lower slowly.

Day 4: Core & Stability

Pallof Press – 12–15 reps (Core, Obliques)
Anchor band at chest height. Hold handles at chest, step sideways to create tension. Press arms straight out, resisting rotation, then return.
Standing Side Crunch – 12–15 each side (Obliques)
Stand tall, band anchored overhead. Pull one elbow down toward hip, engaging obliques, then return slowly.
